Typhoon Marisol makes landfall in Aurora

Typhoon Marisol made landfall in Aurora province at about 8 PM on October 14, 2026, the Republic Weather Bureau said. Maximum sustained winds of 130 km/h were recorded near the center of the storm.

More than 12,000 people were moved to evacuation centers in Aurora and Isabela, according to the National Disaster Office. Tropical Cyclone Wind Signal No. 3 remains raised over Aurora, Isabela, Quezon and Nueva Vizcaya.

The typhoon is moving west-northwest at 15 km/h across northern Luzon. Rainfall of up to 200 mm is expected in the next 24 hours, and officials warned of flooding and landslides in mountain areas.
