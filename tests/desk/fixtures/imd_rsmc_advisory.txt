from ITCZ & hence is likely to intensify further.

Interaction between “Arnab” & low pressure area over southwest Bay of Bengal:
There is low pressure area over southwest Bay of Bengal & adjoining south coastal Tamil Nadu.
Associated scattered to broken, low and medium clouds with embedded intense to very intense
convection lay over the south Arabian Sea, southwest & adjoining westcentral Bay of Bengal,
south interior Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Rayalseema, Lakshadweep Islands area
(Minimum CTT-70 to -90°C) and moderate to intense convection over Kerala (Minimum CTT-50
to -70°C). Distance between the centre of the low pressure area over southwest Bay of Bengal
and cyclone “Arnab” is around 900 km and there is clear cut interaction between the two
systems. As a result, the low pressure area over southwest Bay of Bengal is likely to move east-
southeastwards & cyclone “Arnab” west-northwestwards. While “Arnab” is likely to intensify, the
low pressure area over southwest Bay of Bengal is likely to weaken.

Remarks –
The guidance from various numerical models indicates that the Madden Julian Oscillation (MJO) is
currently in Phase 3 with amplitude more than 1. It is likely to continue in same phase during the
entire forecast period with amplitude more than 1 during next five days. Thus, MJO is favorable for
enhancement of convective activity and further intensification of “Arnab” till landfall over the Bay of
Bengal (BoB).

The guidance from the NCICS model indicates prevalence of westerly wind anomaly (7-9mps)
along with simultaneous occurrence of Equatorial Rossby wave (ERW), Kelvin wave (KW) &
Madden Julian Oscillation (MJO) wave over south and adjoining central BoB and easterly wind
anomaly (7-9 mps) over north BoB. These features indicate a highly favorable environment for its
further intensification into a VSCS over the central BoB till 27th Oct.

The Sea surface temperature is 30°C over eastcentral and adjoining Westcentral BoB and around
29°C along the east coast of India. The ocean heat content is around 80-100 KJ/cm2 over most
parts of Bay of Bengal with 100-120 KJ/cm2 over some pockets over southwest and adjoining
westcentral Bay of Bengal.

The positive low-level vorticity is the same during past 3 hours and is around 200 X10-6 s-1 to the
south of system area with vertical extension upto 200 hPa. The low level vorticity is circularly
oriented and is interacting with the vorticity associated with the low pressure area over southwest
Bay of Bengal. The low level convergence has increased and is around 20X10-6 s-1 to the south of
system centre. Upper-level divergence is around 20X10-6 s-1 to the south of system centre. Mid-
level wind-shear (500-850 hpa) is moderate (5-10) over the system area and along the predicted
path, which is highly favorable for further intensification of the system. Similarly, the deep layer
wind-shear (200-850 hpa) is low to moderate (10-15 kt) over system area and along the expected
path. It is likely to favor further intensification of the system. However, the vertical wind shear is
high over Andhra Pradesh & over adjoining land areas. It would lead to rapid weakening of the
system after landfall.

The upper tropospheric ridge runs along 20-21°N. System has come under the influence of upper
tropospheric easterly winds prevailing to the south of ridge and is therefore likely to be steered
west-northwestwards till 27th evening and then northwestwards till landfall. Thereafter it will be
steered northwards by the southerly winds prevailing in the outer periphery of the ridge to the
northeast of system centre.

A deep trough in westerly is seen along 67 °E to the north of 15 °N at 200 hPa level. It is expected
to reach 80°E on 28th. It would restrict the westward movement of the system after landfall and
would also support northwards re-curvature of the system after landfall.

The Total Precipitable water (TPW) indicates warm moist air (75mm) incursion into the system

Andhra, Odisha and Chhattisgarh region will lead to rapid weakening of the system after landfall
on 28th when the system moves across these areas.

Thus, a favorable environment is prevailing for further intensification of the system (high SST, low
wind shear, simultaneous occurrence of MJO. ERW, KW, pole-ward outflow).

intensity is varying between 50-70kt. Landfall time is varying between 28/00 to 28/12 UTC.
