Sub: - Severe Cyclonic Storm “Murasu” [Pronunciation: Murasu] crossed south Andhra
Pradesh coast [Cyclone Warning for Andhra Pradesh and adjoining north Tamil Nadu
coasts and Post Landfall Outlook —Red Message]
The Severe Cyclonic Storm “Murasu” [Pronunciation: Murasu]” over Westcentral Bay of Bengal close to
south Andhra Pradesh coast moved north-northwestwards with a speed of 08 kmph during past 06 hours,
crossed south Andhra Pradesh coast between Nellore and Narasapur close to Ongole (near Latitude
15.53°N and Longitude 80.22°E) during 0330 to 0530 hours IST with the wind speed of 100-110 kmph
gusting to 120 kmph, and lay centered at 0530 hours IST of today the 03rd November, 2026 over south
coastal Andhra Pradesh, near Latitude 15.6°N and Longitude 80.2°E close to Ongole, about 40 km
southwest of Bapatla (Andhra Pradesh), 80 km north-northeast of Kavali (Andhra Pradesh), and 120 km
west-southwest of Machilipatnam (Andhra Pradesh) and 130 km north of Nellore (Andhra Pradesh),
It is likely to continue to move North-northwestwards and weaken gradually into a cyclonic storm (wind
speed reaching 80-90 kmph gusting to 100 kmph) by noon and further into a deep depression (wind speed
reaching 55-65 kmph gusting to 75 kmph) by midnight of today, the 3rd November,2026.

It is under the continuous surveillance of Doppler Weather Radars at Machilipatnam and Visakhapatnam.

(vi) Damage Expected and Action suggested:
(a) Damage Expected over Coastal Andhra Pradesh and Action suggested
• Structure:
– Major Damage to thatched houses/huts/ kutcha houses.
– Roof tops may blow off
– Unattached metal sheets may fly
– Potential threat to vulnerable structures, tin house, asbestos house.
– Potential threat to structures from detached hoardings, uprooting of trees, breaking of branches
• Communication and Power:
– Minor damage to power and communication poles.
– Breaking of tree branches, uprooting of large avenue trees.
• Agriculture:
– Major damage to standing and coastal crops, plantations, orchards, falling of green coconuts,
tearing of palm fronds. Major damage to banana, Papaya and drumstick trees.
– Uprooting of trees and breaking of tree branches
– Damage to horticulture and standing crops.
– Damage to embankments/ salt pans.
• Marine interest:
– Very high Sea with wave height of 9-14 metre, Visibility severely affected
– Movement in boats/ships unsafe
• Roads/Rail:
– Major damage to Kutcha and Pucca roads.
– Flooding of escape routes. Check for traffic congestion on your route before leaving for your
destination.
– Localized Flooding of roads, water logging in low lying areas.
– Disruption of traffic in major cities due to water logging in roads leading to increased travel time.
– Disruption of railways, overhead powerlines and signalling systems.
• Drinking water/sanitation and human/animal health.
• Secondary hazards: Riverine flooding, Urban flooding, coastal flooding, possible landslides in
hilly areas
Action Suggested
• Total suspension of fishing operations.
• Coastal hutment dwellers to be moved to safer places. People in affected areas to remain indoors.
• Avoid staying in vulnerable structure.
• Judicious regulation of rail and road traffic
• Follow any traffic advisories that are issued in this regard.
• Avoid going to areas that face the water logging problems often.
• Movement in motor boats and small ships unsafe
• Total suspension of offshore/onshore operations
• Fisheries Departments and fishing harbor authorities are advised to alert fishermen and monitor
subsequent IMD marine warnings. Fishermen should plan their activities in accordance with the
latest area-specific advisories till 3rd November.
• Shipping, port and offshore operators are advised to review operational preparedness and monitor
subsequent forecasts for possible restrictions on weather-sensitive activities till 3rd November.
• Maritime authorities may review communication, vessel-tracking and search-and-rescue
preparedness, particularly for vessels operating in areas likely to be affected till 3rd November.
• The extent and timing of restrictions on fishing, shipping, port and marine tourism activities should be
guided by subsequent forecasts and warnings issued by IMD till 3rd November.
