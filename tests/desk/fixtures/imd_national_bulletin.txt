Sub:Cyclonic Storm “Arnab” [Pronunciation : Ornab] over Westcentral and
adjoining Eastcentral Bay of Bengal [Cyclone Warning for Andhra Pradesh and
adjoining South Odisha coasts—Red Message]

The cyclonic storm “Arnab” [Pronunciation :Ornab] over Westcentral and adjoining Eastcentral Bay of
Bengal moved west-northwestwards with a speed of 13 kmph during past 6 hours, and lay centered at
1430 hrs IST of today, the 26th October, 2026 over the same region near latitude 15.5°N and longitude
87.8°E, 520 km southeast of Gopalpur (Odisha), 540 km east-southeast of Vishakhapatnam (Andhra
Pradesh), 620 km east-southeast of Kakinada (Andhra Pradesh), 720 km east-southeast of
Machilipatnam (Andhra Pradesh), 790 km east-southeast of Bapatla (Andhra Pradesh) and 680 km
northwest of Sri Vijaya Puram (Andaman Islands),
It is very likely to move west-northwestwards, intensify further into a severe cyclonic storm over
westcentral Bay of Bengal by evening of today, the 26th October and into a very severe cyclonic storm
over the same region on 27th October.
Thereafter, it is very likely to move northwestwards and cross Andhra Pradesh & Yanam coasts between
Bapatla and Visakhapatnam close to Yanam as a very severe cyclonic storm with the maximum sustained
wind speed of 115-125 kmph gusting to 140 kmph by forenoon of 28th October, 2026.

(vi) Impact Expected and Action Suggested
(a) Impact Expected over Coastal areas of Andhra Pradesh on 27th & 28th October and Action
Suggested
• Structure:
– Total destruction of thatched houses/ extensive damage to kutcha houses.
– Some damage to pucca houses.
– Potential threat from flying objects. Damage to embankments/ salt pans.
• Communication and Power:
– Bending/ uprooting of power and communication poles.
– Breaking of tree branches, uprooting of large avenue trees.
• Agriculture:
– Widespread damage to standing crops, plantations, orchards, falling of green coconuts, tearing
of palm fronds. Widespread damage to banana, Papaya and drumstick trees.
– Blowing down of bushy trees like mango.
– Damage to horticulture and standing crops. Major damage to coastal crops.
• Marine interest:
– Phenomenal Sea with wave height of 14 metre or more, Visibility severely affected
– Movement in boats/ships unsafe
– Small boats, country crafts may get detached from moorings.
• Roads/Rail:
– Major damage to Kutcha and Pucca roads.
– Flooding of escape routes. Check for traffic congestion on your route before leaving for your
destination.
– Localized Flooding of roads, water logging in low lying areas.
– Disruption of traffic in major cities due to water logging in roads leading to increased travel time.
– Major disruption of railways, overhead powerlines and signalling systems.
• Drinking water/sanitation and human/animal health.
• Secondary hazards: Riverine flooding, Urban flooding, coastal flooding, possible landslides in
hilly areas
Action Suggested
• Total suspension of fishing operations.
• Mobilize evacuation from coastal areas. Coastal hutment dwellers to be moved to safer places.
People in affected areas to remain indoors.
• Avoid staying in vulnerable structure.
• Judicious regulation of rail and road traffic
• Follow any traffic advisories that are issued in this regard.
• Avoid going to areas that face the water logging problems often.
• Movement in motor boats and small ships unsafe
• Total suspension of offshore/onshore operations
• Fisheries Departments and fishing harbor authorities are advised to alert fishermen and monitor
subsequent IMD marine warnings. Fishermen should plan their activities in accordance with the
latest area-specific advisories during 25th-28th October.
• Shipping, port and offshore operators are advised to review operational preparedness and monitor
subsequent forecasts for possible restrictions on weather-sensitive activities during 25th -28th Oct.
• Maritime authorities may review communication, vessel-tracking and search-and-rescue
preparedness, particularly for vessels operating in areas likely to be affected during 25th-28th Oct.
• The extent and timing of restrictions on fishing, shipping, port and marine tourism activities should
be guided by subsequent forecasts and warnings issued by IMD during 25th -28th October.
