Champi Audience Trend

Both markets grew more than tenfold as Champi closed in. Taiwan's live-stream audience jumped from 192k views on Oct 20 to 2.8M on Oct 21; Japan's from 19k to 230k by Oct 22. Friday, Oct 23, Taiwan's typhoon day off and Champi's closest pass, isn't measured yet.

Taiwan (Mandarin streams naming 薔琵)

Mid-size channels such as CTi's 新聞龍捲風 took 54–60% of the big days, and the big networks most of the rest. Channels under 100k reached 5% on Oct 22.

Wikipedia, Chinese Champi article: 270 views on Oct 20 → 2,828 on Oct 21. English article: 1,474 → 3,353.

Japan (Japanese streams naming 台風30号)

Until Oct 21 the market belonged to small channels, our own two streams included. On Oct 22, as Champi reached Okinawa, Weathernews and the networks took 95%.

Wikipedia, Japanese 2026 typhoon-season article (Champi has no article of its own): 331 views on Oct 18 → 925 on Oct 21.

How to read this

  • Each bar adds up the views of the Champi live streams that started that day (local time), as counted at 14:42 UTC on Oct 22. Views keep accumulating after a stream ends, so recent days are understated.
  • Oct 22 is partial: it was measured at 22:42 Taiwan time and 23:42 Japan time, and streams still live then are not included.
  • Streams count only when the title names the storm (薔琵 or 台風30号) in Chinese or Japanese script. YouTube search returns at most 50 streams per market, so totals are floors.
