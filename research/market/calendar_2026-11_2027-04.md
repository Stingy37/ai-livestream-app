## C. Geomagnetic storms

Solar cycle 25 is declining (monthly sunspot number 60 in Sep 2026). Past declining phases gave 0–10 G3+ days in a
Nov–Apr window; storms cluster near the March equinox, and aurora in Japan generally needs G4+. Storms cannot be scheduled:
1–3 days of warning for CMEs, ~27 days for recurring coronal holes (SWPC 27-day outlook: G1 Oct 31–Nov 1, repeats possible
~Nov 27–28 and ~Dec 24–25, unconfirmed).

## Typhoons

Champi (2630) passes Sakishima / NE Taiwan Oct 23–24 and weakens toward Kyushu ~Oct 25. After it the Japan season is
effectively over (normals: Nov 0.5 approaches, Dec 0.1). PAGASA expects 2–4 more cyclones Nov–Apr (below normal, strong El
Niño), so late Philippine landfalls are possible but fewer.
