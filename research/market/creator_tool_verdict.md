# Selling the livestream system to creators: verdict (2026-10-22)

Research agent findings (web sources, unverified items marked) plus our own YouTube measurements. The full report with all
source links is in the Claude session of 2026-10-22; key sources are linked below.

## Verdict
- **Software for small creators: not viable.** Only ~30 of 486 small channels in our data streamed 2+ events; at their view
  counts (median ~4,700 total) ad revenue (Japan ~$2.5–3 RPM, Mexico ~$0.6–1.5, Philippines ~$0.2–0.5; low-confidence
  aggregator figures) can't cover a ~$30/mo tool. A template sold to many channels is the "mass production" pattern YouTube's
  inauthentic-content policy targets, so one enforcement wave could hit every customer at once.
- **Possible market: broadcasters, publishers, multilingual civic channels.** Scripps is moving ~12 stations to automated 24/7
  local streams; The Weather Company launched AI scripts for TV stations (Oct 20 2026, human approval before air); QAB Okinawa,
  GMA and FTV run AI presenters. Product shape: white-label, human approval gate, official-source citations, per-event or
  retainer pricing.
- **Or keep it as our own media channels.**

## Platform and legal risks (apply to our own channel too)
- YouTube "inauthentic content" (Jul 2025, clarified Jul 2026) covers live streams; automation is allowed only if the result
  shows real educational/commentary value. https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392
- Natural disasters are "sensitive events": YouTube may limit ads on related content. https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6162278
- TikTok LIVE bans AI-generated voices in live streams (framed around shopping; scope unclear).
- Japan: Meteorological Service Act amendment (May 29 2026) extends forecast-licence rules to foreign services; narration must
  relay JMA forecasts, never make its own. https://www.jma.go.jp/jma/kishou/books/hakusho/2026/index2.html
- Government data (JMA, CWA) is free for commercial use with attribution; commercial Himawari data goes through JMBSC; our
  real per-customer costs would be LLM/TTS/GPU compute.

## Competitors
24/7 stream hosts (Upstream $30, LiveReacting $40, Gyre $49, OneStream, Restream, StreamYard) only play files; AI video/voice
(HeyGen, ElevenLabs, InVideo) don't do live data; Futuri AudioAI does AI radio. No off-the-shelf product makes a fact-checked,
multilingual, data-driven live event stream. Weathernews LiVE (1.55M subs, human presenters) is the incumbent in Japan.
